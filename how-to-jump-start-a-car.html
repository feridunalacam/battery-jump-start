How to Jump-Start a Car

A practical, safety-first guide

Connecting jumper cables incorrectly can damage a vehicle or cause injury. If you are unsure, stop and call a professional.

What You Need

  • A vehicle with a charged 12-volt battery or a suitable jump pack
  • Undamaged, correctly rated jumper cables
  • Eye protection and a safe, ventilated location

Before You Begin

Check the owner’s manual. Do not attempt a jump start if the battery is cracked, leaking, frozen, swollen, or visibly damaged. Keep flames and sparks away.

Connect the Cables in This Order

  1. Turn both vehicles off and set the parking brakes.
  2. Connect red to the dead battery’s positive terminal.
  3. Connect the other red clamp to the good battery’s positive terminal.
  4. Connect black to the good battery’s negative terminal.
  5. Connect the final black clamp to an unpainted grounding point away from the battery.

Start the Vehicles

Start the assisting vehicle, wait briefly, then try the disabled vehicle. Once started, disconnect the cables in reverse order without allowing the clamps to touch.
